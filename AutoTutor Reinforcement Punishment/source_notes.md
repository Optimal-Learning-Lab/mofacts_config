# Source Notes: Reinforcement vs. punishment

Source roles satisfy the module requirement for at least one authoritative source, one misconception or learning-difficulty source, and one examples/problems source.

## REINF_OPENSTAX
- Role: authoritative/examples
- Title: OpenStax Psychology 2e 6.3: Operant Conditioning
- URL: https://openstax.org/books/psychology-2e/pages/6-3-operant-conditioning
- Notes used: Defines positive/negative as adding/removing stimuli and reinforcement/punishment as increasing/decreasing behavior; gives seatbelt, praise, scolding, and toy-removal examples.

## REINF_IHS
- Role: misconception/learning difficulty
- Title: Indian Health Service ABA slides: Common Myths and Misconceptions about ABA
- URL: https://www.ihs.gov/sites/telebehavioral/themes/responsive2017/display_objects/documents/slides/othertopics/abaforasd0516.pdf
- Notes used: Explicitly notes the misconception that negative reinforcement means punishment.

## REINF_SIMPLY
- Role: examples/problems
- Title: Simply Psychology: Operant Conditioning
- URL: https://www.simplypsychology.org/operant-conditioning.html
- Notes used: Provides accessible examples differentiating positive reinforcement, negative reinforcement, positive punishment, and negative punishment.
