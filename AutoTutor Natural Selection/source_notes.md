# Source Notes: Natural selection

Source roles satisfy the module requirement for at least one authoritative source, one misconception or learning-difficulty source, and one examples/problems source.

## SEL_OPENSTAX
- Role: authoritative/examples
- Title: OpenStax Biology 2e 18.1 and 19.3
- URL: https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/18-1-understanding-evolution
- Notes used: States Darwin's principles for natural selection, gives finch examples, and warns that evolution has no goal of becoming bigger, faster, or smarter.

## SEL_GREGORY
- Role: misconception/learning difficulty
- Title: Gregory: Understanding Natural Selection: Essential Concepts and Common Misconceptions
- URL: https://evolution-outreach.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1007/s12052-009-0128-1
- Notes used: Reviews widespread misconceptions: need-based change, intentional adaptation, individuals evolving, and inaccurate evolutionary language.

## SEL_SMITHSONIAN
- Role: misconception/examples
- Title: Smithsonian Science Education Center: Natural Selection Common Misconceptions
- URL: https://ssec.si.edu/natural-selection-common-misconceptions
- Notes used: Catalogs common classroom pitfalls and supports non-teleological, population-level explanations.

## SEL_OPENSTAX_POP
- Role: authoritative
- Title: OpenStax Biology 2e 19.2: Population Genetics
- URL: https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/19-2-population-genetics
- Notes used: Explains that selection acts only on heritable variation and environmental differences are not inherited in the same way.
