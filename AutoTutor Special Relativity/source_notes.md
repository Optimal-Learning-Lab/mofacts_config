# Source Notes: Special relativity

Source roles satisfy the module requirement for at least one authoritative source, one misconception or learning-difficulty source, and one examples/problems source.

## REL_OPENSTAX_TIME
- Role: authoritative/examples
- Title: OpenStax University Physics Volume 3 5.3: Time Dilation
- URL: https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-3/pages/5-3-time-dilation
- Notes used: Defines time dilation using proper time and inertial frames; supports light-clock and high-speed examples.

## REL_OPENSTAX_CONSEQ
- Role: authoritative/examples
- Title: OpenStax Physics 10.2: Consequences of Special Relativity
- URL: https://openstax.org/books/physics/pages/10-2-consequences-of-special-relativity
- Notes used: Introduces inertial frames, time dilation, and empirical confirmations such as orbiting atomic clocks.

## REL_VOKOS
- Role: misconception/learning difficulty
- Title: Vokos et al.: Student understanding of time in special relativity
- URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/physics/0207109
- Notes used: Reports student difficulties with simultaneity and reference frames after standard instruction.

## REL_EINSTEIN_ONLINE
- Role: examples/problems
- Title: Einstein Online: Special relativity spotlights
- URL: https://www.einstein-online.info/en/complete_spotlights/
- Notes used: Accessible conceptual examples for relativity of simultaneity and time dilation.

## Traceability
- Expectations and misconceptions cite these source keys in their `sourceSupport` arrays.
- Core expectations are atomic and each maps to at least one listed source.
- Misconceptions are authored only where the source notes identify a documented or commonly taught learning difficulty.
