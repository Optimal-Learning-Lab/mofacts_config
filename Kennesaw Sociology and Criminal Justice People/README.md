# Kennesaw Sociology and Criminal Justice People

Name-recall lesson with 51 cards, one per faculty/staff member with a downloaded portrait. Each stimulus uses the standard `display.imgSrc` plus `display.text`: portrait first, then role title, then research interests. Missing research interests are an empty second paragraph. Expected responses contain the listed first and last names without middle initials; compound names are preserved.

## Session and import

The Instructions and Practice units, adaptive probability equation, speech settings, study/drill/review timing, and practice duration match `Memphis Psychology People`. `stimuliPosition: "left"` uses the existing split layout: portrait above role/research text, with the response panel alongside on larger screens. There is no sequential timed reveal of the two stimulus components.

Import the adjacent `Kennesaw Sociology and Criminal Justice People.zip` through the normal MoFaCTS package upload. The ZIP contains two JSON files and 51 images at its root. This is a new lesson, so `tdfId` is intentionally omitted; the first import assigns its identity. Retain the canonical package exported after import before future updates. No existing lesson identity or learner history is reused.

## Sources and coverage

The source is the faculty/staff directory collected September 6, 2026, supplemented by linked faculty portraits, the chair appointment announcement, the research page, and the faculty spotlight page. Research text includes the directory's stated areas of expertise and teaching interests. Roles retain listed coordinator/director duties. The directory's duplicated surname for Darina Lepadatu was normalized during collection. Dean Dabney's role follows the administration listing and August 1, 2026 appointment, rather than the outdated introductory welcome.

Original downloaded image bytes are preserved; the largest available image by pixel area is selected per person. Neutral package filenames avoid revealing the answer through the filename. The university pages did not establish an open reuse license; no license grant is asserted here. Original image ownership is unchanged.

Excluded because no portrait was available in the collected data: David Ayeni, Lynnette Coto, Christopher Dale, Gary Killam, Nicole Lehner, Lauren Lunk, Nathan McCreary, Montana Nowak, Jennifermichele Petersen, Phyllis Russell, Heidi Scherer, Shari Sheridan, Frank Starosto. No placeholder portrait or text-only replacement was created.
