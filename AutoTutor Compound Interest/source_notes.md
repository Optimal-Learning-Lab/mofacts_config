# Source Notes: Compound interest

Source roles satisfy the module requirement for at least one authoritative source, one misconception or learning-difficulty source, and one examples/problems source.

## CMP_OPENSTAX
- Role: authoritative/examples
- Title: OpenStax Contemporary Mathematics 6.4: Compound Interest
- URL: https://openstax.org/books/contemporary-mathematics/pages/6-4-compound-interest
- Notes used: Defines compound interest as interest added to principal so later interest is calculated on principal plus accumulated interest; includes worked annual compounding examples and simple-vs-compound contrasts.

## CMP_CFPB
- Role: authoritative
- Title: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau: How does compound interest work?
- URL: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/how-does-compound-interest-work-en-1683/
- Notes used: Consumer-facing explanation of compounding with examples; supports plain-language framing of interest earning interest.

## CMP_MCKENZIE_LIERSCH
- Role: misconception/learning difficulty
- Title: McKenzie & Liersch: Misunderstanding Savings Growth
- URL: https://psy2.ucsd.edu/~mckenzie/McKenzie%26LierschInPressJMR.pdf
- Notes used: Documents that people often underestimate savings growth by linearizing exponential growth; supports the linear-growth misconception.

## CMP_KHAN
- Role: examples/problems
- Title: Khan Academy: Compound interest
- URL: https://www.khanacademy.org/math/compound-interest
- Notes used: Practice problems and solved examples using principal, rate, compounding period, amount, and interest.
