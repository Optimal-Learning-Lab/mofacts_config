# Source Notes: Stock shorting

Source roles satisfy the module requirement for at least one authoritative source, one misconception or learning-difficulty source, and one examples/problems source.

## SHORT_SEC_REGSHO
- Role: authoritative
- Title: SEC: Key Points About Regulation SHO
- URL: https://www.sec.gov/investor/pubs/regsho.htm
- Notes used: Defines short selling as selling borrowed stock and later buying to return it; describes uses including decline speculation, liquidity, and hedging.

## SHORT_SCHWAB
- Role: examples/problems
- Title: Schwab: The Ins and Outs of Short Selling
- URL: https://www.schwab.com/learn/story/ins-and-outs-short-selling
- Notes used: Provides concrete short-sale examples and emphasizes risks when the stock rises.

## SHORT_CRS
- Role: authoritative/misconception
- Title: Congressional Research Service: Short Selling: Background and Policy Issues
- URL: https://www.congress.gov/crs-products/product/pdf/IF/IF12400/3
- Notes used: Explains borrowing, selling, returning shares, policy concerns, and that manipulative short-and-distort conduct is distinct from ordinary short selling.

## SHORT_BANKRATE
- Role: examples/problems
- Title: Bankrate: What is short interest?
- URL: https://www.bankrate.com/investing/what-is-short-interest/
- Notes used: Explains unlimited-loss intuition: stock price can keep rising while maximum gain is bounded by a fall to zero.
