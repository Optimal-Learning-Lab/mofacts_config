Approved revision applied to localhost on October 15, 2026. Both existing lesson identities now contain this 40-KC inventory. Earlier history and all shared-library entries are preserved. The labels below map to the matching suffixes in the revised KC identities.

Author’s rules applied

Examples instantiate KCs and remain supporting content. A named example, its organism names, location, dates, or measurements is never a separate KC or mandatory case to complete. Cautions and false generalizations are handled as diagnostic misconceptions rather than positive mastery requirements. Terms may be fact KCs. A KC can combine fact, model, and rule knowledge when those elements form one coherent demand. Model plus rule is a principle. Existing prerequisite biology supports the new chapter connections; it is not independently added to the required inventory.

Complete retained misconception inventory

The existing M20 requirement about conclusive proof from matching trees is excluded from this revision because the chapter audit did not establish that additional demand. M17’s corrective category becomes fact-rule: the revised target asks for origin classification, not a separate explanation of every polyploid mechanism. The other 18 diagnostic statements and categories are retained.

Author-added requirements removed without promotion

The unmatched warnings about intentional choice of barriers, universal numerical thresholds, and proof from matching trees are not automatically converted into new misconceptions. Their presence in our earlier wording does not establish course authorization. The extra polyploid chromosome-pairing synthesis is removed. General tree-building and detailed sequence-analysis skills are not introduced as new Chapter 21 prerequisites or mastery targets.
