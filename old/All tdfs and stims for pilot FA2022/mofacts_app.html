Helpful Tips

    • Sometimes, you will be given the first part of the keyword as a hint; you may type either the entire keyword or the remaining part of the keyword as your answer.

    • You can change the settings so that the computer reads the question out loud to you; you can adjust the speed of the computer reading out loud by changing the speed from Normal to 1.25 or higher (faster) or to 0.75 or lower (slower)

    • Click on the Progress button to see how completely you have covered the material.

    • There is a timer on each question; this timer is for your benefit so you don't waste time if you can't recall. Remember that this is not a test, and it is okay to get questions wrong! When your answer is wrong, the system will wait before it gives you a new question, so you can read the feedback before continuing.

    • If you have any problems, click on the Report Error button; this will automatically send the programmers and project lead an email about your issue

Feedback Settings

When an answer is incorrect, the system gives the correct answer.

When an answer is incorrect, the system provides additional explanation about why that answer is wrong.

When an answer is incorrect, the system asks you follow up questions to help you remember the differences between your answer and the correct answer.


*Refutational and Dialogue feedback are only activated for some questions.
